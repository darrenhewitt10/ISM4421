# Map data

`basemap-na.json` is the app's own basemap: land, lakes, country and state
borders, major highways and city labels for North America and the Caribbean,
plus coarse world land elsewhere.

- Source: [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:10m and 1:110m
  layers (public domain), clipped to lon -135..-50, lat 5..60 and simplified with
  [mapshaper](https://github.com/mbloch/mapshaper).
- A few smaller South/Central Florida towns (Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Jupiter,
  etc.) were added by hand for local labels.
